EXPERIMENTS\n\n## A. Dataset and Details  \nThe LEVIR-CC [16] and WHU-CDC [25] datasets were utilized for the experimental analysis of RSICC. The LEVIR-CC dataset consists of 1077 image pairs, each accompanied by five annotation captions provided by five different annotators. Given that the bitemporal RSIs in the dataset span a long period and often feature irrelevant changes commonly found in remote sensing imagery, image pairs that exhibit only irrelevant changes are considered as unchanged image pairs. The WHU-CDC dataset comprises 7434 pairs of images, each with dimensions of 256 × 256 pixels and five annotated sentences. The dataset considers the scenes depicted in bitemporal images, identifies the nature of the changes, and considers attributes and relative positions of the changes in relation to other objects. In total, the dataset contains 37 170 sentences.
